HOW RECALRO WORKS

From first upload to exam-day confidence.

Follow one week of class notes through Recalro, from the moment they're uploaded to the day you're ready.

Swipe through the animated journey →

  1. 01

    Upload your study materials

    Drop in lecture notes, PDFs or slides. Recalro reads them and keeps everything for that subject in one place.

    PDFWorld_History_Notes.pdf24 pages · 2.4 MBUploading…Ready · 24 pages read
  2. 02

    AI organises the material

    Pages become topics and a short summary of the key ideas, so you can see the shape of the week before you start.

    Ancient EgyptSilk RoadsMoon landingBerlin Wall
    Summary · 5 key ideasPeople, ideas and events connected across centuries…
  3. 03

    Flashcards are generated

    Each idea becomes a question you have to answer from memory. That effort is what makes it stick.

    You practise recall

    Flip, answer, rate how well you knew it. Every rating teaches the scheduler when to bring the card back.
